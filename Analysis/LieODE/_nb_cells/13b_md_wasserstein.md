### 4. Wasserstein Manifold Analysis

Optimal transport quantifies the difference between Tracking and Playback embedding distributions. Physical interpretation: $W_2^2(\mu_{TR}, \mu_{PB})$ is the minimum "kinetic energy" (control cost) to deform the passive Playback state into the active Tracking state. Unlike the Lie algebra fitting, this does not require $dR/dt$ to be predictable — it only requires the embedding to preserve topological relationships.
